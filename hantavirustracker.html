Transparency & Legal

Transparency about data sources and limitations.

Information Sources

News content is aggregated from publicly available RSS feeds and Google News indexing. The developer does not claim ownership of any third-party news content.

The app only aggregates publicly available information for informational and awareness purposes. Information is refreshed each time the app is opened.

The accuracy, completeness, or reliability of external news sources cannot be guaranteed by the developer. Users are advised to verify critical information through official health organizations and authoritative sources.

News Aggregation

News articles are collected via Google RSS feeds using the keyword "Hantavirus". The app does not create, edit, or verify the news content itself. All news is sourced from third-party publishers indexed by Google.

Informational Purpose Only

Hantavirus Tracker is not a medical service and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. The app is intended solely for informational and awareness purposes.
