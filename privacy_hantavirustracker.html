Privacy Policy for Hantavirus Tracker

Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Hantavirus Tracker ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, processed, and used when you use the Hantavirus Tracker mobile application (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Hantavirus Tracker does not require user accounts and does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as names, personal addresses, uploaded content, or phone numbers.

The App may collect limited anonymous technical and analytics information, including:

  • Analytics Data: Anonymous usage statistics such as app opens, feature usage, screen views, session duration, and interaction metrics.
  • Crash and Performance Data: Technical information related to crashes, device type, operating system version, diagnostics, and app stability.
  • Advertising Identifiers (if enabled in future versions): Non-personal identifiers used for analytics or advertising purposes.

3. How Information Is Collected

Information is collected automatically through third-party analytics, infrastructure, and crash reporting services integrated into the App.

These services may include:

  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google Play Services
  • Google Maps SDK

4. How We Use Information

The limited anonymous information collected is used exclusively to:

  • Monitor app stability and technical performance
  • Detect and fix crashes or technical issues
  • Improve user experience and app usability
  • Understand general usage trends
  • Maintain and improve the App and its features

5. News Content, RSS Feeds, and Third-Party Sources

Hantavirus Tracker functions partially as a news aggregation and informational platform.

The App aggregates publicly available news articles and headlines related to hantavirus and outbreak-related topics from third-party news providers and publicly accessible RSS feeds, including sources indexed through Google News RSS services.

Hantavirus Tracker does not create, edit, rewrite, or independently verify third-party news articles. All news content, trademarks, logos, article titles, and published materials remain the property of their respective publishers, authors, and copyright owners.

Whenever possible, the App displays attribution information including:

  • Original publisher or news source
  • Publication title
  • Publication date
  • Direct links to the original article

Users who open news articles may be redirected to external third-party websites or applications that are not controlled or operated by Hantavirus Tracker.

We are not responsible for:

  • The accuracy or reliability of third-party news content
  • The availability of external websites
  • The privacy practices of third-party publishers
  • The content, modifications, or updates made by external news providers

News content is provided strictly for informational and public awareness purposes only. Users should independently verify important medical, scientific, or public health information through official health organizations and governmental authorities.

6. Map and Statistical Information

The App may display outbreak statistics, case tracking information, and global map visualizations related to hantavirus activity.

Some map and statistical features may be experimental or beta features and may rely on publicly available data sources. Data may be incomplete, delayed, or subject to inaccuracies.

Hantavirus Tracker should not be considered an official medical authority, epidemiological institution, or emergency response service.

7. Data Storage

Hantavirus Tracker does not create user accounts and does not store personal user data on proprietary servers.

Some anonymous analytics and crash-related information may be processed and temporarily stored by third-party infrastructure providers such as Google Firebase.

8. Sharing of Information
