Privacy Policy for Hantavirus Tracker

Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Hantavirus Tracker ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when you use the Hantavirus Tracker mobile application (the "App").

2. Information We Collect

Hantavirus Tracker does not require user accounts and does not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise user-generated content.

The App may collect limited anonymous technical and analytics information, including:

  • Analytics Data: Anonymous usage statistics such as app opens, screen views, session duration, and general interaction metrics.
  • Crash and Performance Data: Technical information related to crashes, errors, device type, operating system version, and performance diagnostics.
  • Advertising Identifiers (if ads are enabled in future versions): Non-personal identifiers used for analytics or advertising purposes.

3. How Information Is Collected

Information is collected automatically through third-party analytics and crash reporting services integrated into the App.

These services may include:

  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google Play Services

4. How We Use Information

The limited information collected is used exclusively to:

  • Monitor app stability and performance
  • Fix crashes and technical issues
  • Improve user experience
  • Understand general app usage trends
  • Maintain and improve the App

5. News Content and External Links

Hantavirus Tracker aggregates publicly available news articles and links from third-party news providers.

When users open a news article, they may be redirected to an external website or browser controlled by third parties. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

6. Data Storage

Hantavirus Tracker does not create user accounts and does not store personal user data on our servers.

Some anonymous analytics and crash-related information may be processed by third-party services such as Firebase.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

Limited anonymous technical data may be shared with:

  • Analytics providers
  • Crash reporting providers
  • Service providers necessary to operate the App
  • Authorities if required by law

8. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services including:

  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • NewsAPI
  • Google Maps

These third-party services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the integrity and security of the App and related services. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure.

10. Children’s Privacy

Hantavirus Tracker is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised update date.
